# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in SekaiDesk a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity, and to keep discussions welcoming and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus on constructive technical feedback.
- Respect the time and bandwidth of maintainers and contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or harassing behavior may be reported through GitHub issue moderation or by contacting the repository maintainer (`@Kyokkei`). Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, lock threads, or block repeat offenders.

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.*
